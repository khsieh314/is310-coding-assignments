## Gotta Scrape them All
The website I will attempt at scraping over is the Bulbapedia pokemon wiki!
It does have a robots.txt at this link: https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/robots.txt

It disallows for random pages ang shipping but allows for scraping over most other pages.

I used to watch the anime with my sister back when I was really young and have recently gotten back into the artwork and other things as well.

For my webscraper, the plan is to scrape both the Kanto regin pokemon and the Unova region pokemon to get information on how the types of pokemon have changed over time and how that might change based on what region you are in.
